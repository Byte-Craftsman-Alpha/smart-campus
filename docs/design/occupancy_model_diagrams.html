Room allocation: the corrected model, in diagrams

Six pictures that explain why "room is free / occupied" fails, and what to build instead: reservations as intervals, availability derived, conflicts enforced by the database, and attendance kept out of allocation entirely.

occupancy_model_diagrams.md holds all seven diagrams as copy-pasteable Mermaid source — this page renders them so you can read them without a runtime.

Diagram 1Why a free/occupied toggle fails

A room is not a switch — it is a timeline of reservations. Here is one morning on three rooms. The hatched block is a class that was cancelled: the booking row still exists with status = CANCELLED, and only the unrest of the window returned to the pool.

The four failures of the toggle model

1 · Cannot answer "free tomorrow 10:00–10:55", only "free now". 2 · Freeing by clearing the record destroys the history requirement 7 demands. 3 · A class that began at 10:00 still occupies the room at 10:20 — mid-period release is worthless in practice. 4 · "0 present" is indistinguishable from "class cancelled", which is exactly the ambiguity that cost students the NSS exam.

Diagram 3One booking can double-book three different things

A room-only check catches the first case and silently permits the other two. Section clash is the one most teams forget — and it is the one students suffer from.

-- 1 · no room is double-booked
EXCLUDE USING gist (room_id     WITH =, time_range WITH &&) WHERE (status IN ('CONFIRMED','PENDING'));

-- 2 · no SECTION sits in two rooms at once   ← the one teams forget
EXCLUDE USING gist (section_id  WITH =, time_range WITH &&) WHERE (status IN ('CONFIRMED','PENDING'));

-- 3 · no FACULTY sits in two rooms at once
EXCLUDE USING gist (requested_by WITH =, time_range WITH &&) WHERE (status IN ('CONFIRMED','PENDING'));

Diagram 4Booking lifecycle — cancel, never delete

Why PENDING must hold the slot

If a pending Seminar-Hall request does not occupy the interval, three faculty can each be "approved" for the same room. Pending bookings therefore participate in the exclusion constraint — they just cannot be completed until approved.

Diagram 5The three operations — and nothing else

Suggestion is a read. Only reservation is a write. That separation is why a stale suggestion can never corrupt the schedule: the database re-decides at insert time.

Diagram 7Attendance is not an allocation signal

The single most important separation in the whole model — and the exact point where the original idea was wrong.

What replaces the "mark 0 present" instinct

1 · Planned cancellation (the one that matters): "tomorrow's P3 is cancelled" → status change + notice to the affected sections + slot becomes bookable. Real reuse is planned, not accidental.

2 · Early release (partial): the faculty member shortens the booking — end_at = now — and only the remainder of the period returns to the pool. Gate it to the booking owner, ≥30 min before start, with an audit event.

3 · No-show handling, if you want it: an admin-only flag after the period ends. It affects reporting only; it never rewrites history.
